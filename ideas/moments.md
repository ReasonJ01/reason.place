# Moments microsite idea

A possible future microsite built around small facets of personality: things that matter because of the particular feeling, atmosphere, rhythm, or quality of experience they create.

This is not simply a site about sounds or scenery. The common thread is small states of being that feel especially good or meaningful — sensory moments, kinds of company, weather, light, quiet, movement, and ways of relating.

Working name: **Moments**. Earlier framing: **Atmospheres**.

## Examples

- The sound of heavy rain on a flat roof.
- Headlights, brake lights, and streetlights reflected in puddles on a dark winter evening while it is pouring rain at 5pm.
- Sunlight dancing over the surface of water and glistening like brilliant diamonds.
- The lack of sound during a light midnight snowfall.
- Running water.
- Birds in a forest.
- The first sounds of traffic and birds in the early hours of the morning.
- A thunderstorm that can be felt in your chest.
- The endless babble of a small child.
- A group of friends around a firepit, silhouetted by the flames against the night.
- The sweetness and ease of a flowing text-message chain where each person drops back in whenever they get the chance.

## Broader reason.place idea

The collection of microsites should represent small, deliberate facets of personality and things that matter, rather than being a set of generic catalogues.

Examples already present or being explored:

- Recipes — what you like to make and eat.
- Scents — taste in fragrance and materials.
- Postcards — places that stuck with you and the memories attached to them.
- Moments — small sensory, social, and atmospheric states that you value.

A useful filter for future microsites: each one should reveal a distinct part of taste, experience, curiosity, or values; it should not exist merely because the subject can be catalogued.
