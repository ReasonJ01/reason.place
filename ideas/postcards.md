# Postcards microsite idea

A possible future `postcards.reason.place` microsite: a personal visual travel/experience archive built from places worth remembering. The emphasis should be on a small number of high-quality entries rather than a comprehensive travel log.

Potential entry shape:

- place
- trip / location
- one image
- date or approximate year
- one short note
